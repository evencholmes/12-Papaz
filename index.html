About
Papaz - The ExamOS
Version 2.0 - Your academic command center
Papaz was built for every student and professional who has ever stared at a syllabus and felt lost. It tracks what you know, calls you out when you disappear, and helps you finish what you started.
All data lives on your device only. Nothing is ever sent to a server. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.
